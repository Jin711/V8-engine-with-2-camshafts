The four strokes

Two crank revolutions make a cycle, 720 degrees.

  1. 01Intake

    The piston drops with the intake valve open; low pressure pulls in air and fuel.

  2. 02Compression

    Valves shut as the piston squeezes the charge; the plug fires just before the top.

  3. 03Power

    Burning gas pushes the piston down, the one stroke that adds energy.

  4. 04Exhaust

    The exhaust valve opens and the rising piston pushes the spent gas out.

Firing order

Each cylinder fires once per cycle, so the crank gets a push every 90 degrees.

Left bank, cylinders 1, 3, 5, 7

    Right bank, cylinders 2, 4, 6, 8

      The left bank, 1, 3, 5, 7 front to back, is the driver’s side; 2, 4, 6, 8 sit on the right.

      The cross-plane crankshaft sets its four throws 90° apart: that is the even beat of a V8, and the uneven exhaust pulses behind its burble.
